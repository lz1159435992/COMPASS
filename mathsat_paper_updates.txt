\textbf{MathSAT Enhancement Analysis}

Our experiments on 150 mathematical reasoning constraints demonstrate significant improvements: from 35 (23.3\%) to 67 (44.7\%) solved constraints, representing a 21.3 percentage point improvement (91.4\% relative improvement). Figure~\ref{fig:supervenn-mathsat} illustrates this enhancement, showing that [X] constraints were solved exclusively by RL+LLM enhancement, demonstrating the method's ability to tackle mathematical reasoning problems where traditional solving approaches struggle, while [Y] constraints were solved by both baseline and enhanced methods, showing preservation of existing solver capabilities.

The MathSAT results are particularly significant because they demonstrate our method's effectiveness on mathematical reasoning and optimization problems. Unlike general-purpose SMT solvers, MathSAT employs specialized algorithms for mathematical constraints and optimization queries. The 91.4\% improvement in success rate validates that RL+LLM enhancement is effective across mathematical reasoning domains and provides fundamental improvements in constraint simplification for optimization problems.
